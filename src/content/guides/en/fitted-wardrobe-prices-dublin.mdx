## What drives the price

**Width and height.** Price scales with the length of the run. Very tall rooms — common in Rathmines, Rathgar and other period homes — need top sections or extra doors.

**Doors and finish.** Woodgrain and matt melamine doors are the most affordable. Spray-painted MDF in any colour costs more, and real oak or walnut veneer more again. Mirrored and glass panels sit in between.

**The interior.** Hanging rails and shelves are included. Drawers, shoe racks, trouser pull-outs and LED lighting are where budgets grow — and where a wardrobe becomes genuinely useful.

**The room.** Sloped ceilings, chimney breasts, bay windows and uneven walls take more design and fitting time, but they are exactly where made-to-measure beats any off-the-shelf system.

**Access.** Apartments with small lifts or management-company rules, and city-centre parking, add time. Our area pages show how travel and access affect prices in your part of Dublin.

## Hinged or sliding doors?

Hinged doors give you full access to the whole wardrobe at once and suit traditional rooms. Sliding doors save floor space in narrow bedrooms and box rooms, and work beautifully with mirrors, but you can only open half the wardrobe at a time. As a rule, if there is less than about 70 cm between the wardrobe and the bed, sliding doors are the better choice.

## Fitted or flat-pack?

Flat-pack systems are cheaper to buy, and if your room is a simple rectangle they can work well — we assemble and anchor them from <Price s="furniture-assembly" i="wardrobeLarge" /> for modular systems. But a flat-pack wardrobe will rarely reach the ceiling, fit an alcove or follow a sloping wall, and filler panels and gaps are the price you pay. Made-to-measure uses every centimetre.

## Materials and EU standards

Every fitted wardrobe we build uses **E1-class, low-formaldehyde boards**. Since 6 August 2026, EU rules (Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/1464) limit formaldehyde released by furniture to 0.062 mg/m³ — good news for bedroom air quality. We fit soft-close hinges and full-extension runners, and anchor tall units securely to the wall. Read more on our standards page.
