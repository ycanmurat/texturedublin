Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use as few as possible, and **analytics and advertising cookies are only set after you say yes**. "Reject all" is always as easy as "Accept all", and you can change your mind at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.

## Cookies and similar storage we use

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Type | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `td-consent` (local storage) | Texture Dublin | Remembers your cookie choices | Strictly necessary | 6 months |
| `td-wa-bubble` (session storage) | Texture Dublin | Remembers you have seen the WhatsApp tip | Strictly necessary | End of visit |
| `td-admin-session` | Texture Dublin | Secure sign-in for the product administrator only | Strictly necessary | 8 hours |
| `_ga`, `_ga_<ID>` | Google Analytics 4 | Counts visits and pages anonymously so we can improve the site | Analytics — consent | Up to 2 years |
| `_gcl_au` | Google Ads | Measures whether our Google ads lead to enquiries | Marketing — consent | 90 days |
| `_fbp`, `_fbc` | Meta (Facebook / Instagram) | Measures our Facebook and Instagram ads and shows relevant ads | Marketing — consent | 90 days |

## Google Consent Mode

We use Google Consent Mode v2. Until you consent, Google tags are not loaded at all. If you accept analytics or marketing, Google receives your choice and only uses cookies for the purposes you allowed.

## Fonts and other third parties

Our fonts are hosted on our own server, so visiting this website does not send your IP address to Google Fonts. When you click through to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or Google Maps, those services set their own cookies under their own policies.

## Managing cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage may mean we ask for your cookie choice on every visit.

For how we use personal data more generally, see our privacy policy.
