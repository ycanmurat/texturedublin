## WhatsApp

When you message us on WhatsApp, the service is provided by WhatsApp Ireland Limited, which processes your messages under its own terms and privacy policy. The quote form on this website does not store anything: it simply opens WhatsApp (or your email app) with your message filled in. Messages are kept on our business phone for as long as we need them for your enquiry or project.

## Who we share data with

Only with the people who help us deliver the service, and only what they need:

- trusted trades working on your project (for example a Safe Electric registered electrician for an illuminated sign);
- our accountant and Revenue, for tax purposes;
- IT providers who process data on our behalf: website hosting, email, Google Ireland Limited (Analytics and Ads) and Meta Platforms Ireland Limited (Meta Pixel), when you have consented.

Some of these providers may transfer data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep data

- Enquiries that don't lead to a project: up to 12 months.
- Customer, quote and invoice records: six years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as Irish tax law requires.
- Google Analytics data: 14 months.
- Your cookie choice: six months, after which we ask again.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, or to withdraw your consent at any time (use **Cookie settings** in the footer for cookies). Contact us at <a href={`mailto:${site.email}`}>{site.email}</a> — we reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the **Data Protection Commission**, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28 — [www.dataprotection.ie](https://www.dataprotection.ie).

## Security

We keep devices and accounts protected with passwords and two-factor authentication, share data only when needed and use providers that apply appropriate security measures. This website is served over HTTPS.

## Children

Our services are not aimed at people under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
