We want everyone to be able to find our prices, read our advice and contact us easily. This website is designed to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA**, the standard referenced by EN 301 549 and the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882).

## What we have done

- Semantic headings, landmarks and a "skip to content" link on every page.
- Full keyboard navigation with visible focus outlines, including menus, maps, accordions and the estimator.
- Text alternatives for meaningful graphics; decorative drawings are hidden from screen readers.
- Colour contrast that meets AA for text, and layouts that work when zoomed to 200% or viewed on small screens.
- Animations respect the "reduce motion" setting on your device, and no content flashes.
- The whole website is available in English and in Irish (Gaeilge), with the correct language marked for assistive technology.
- Forms have visible labels, and the quote form works without a mouse.

## Known limitations

- The interactive area map is a visual summary; every area on it is also listed as a normal link on the areas page.
- Some third-party services we link to (WhatsApp, social media) are outside our control.

## Feedback

If anything on this website is difficult to use, please tell us and we will provide the information in another way: <ContactLink />. We aim to reply within five working days.

This statement was prepared on 27 October 2026 based on a self-assessment and is reviewed whenever the website changes significantly.
